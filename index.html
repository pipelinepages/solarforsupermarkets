Frequently asked questions

Before you book the assessment.

Can solar run supermarket freezers and refrigerators?

Yes, when the system is properly designed for the equipment's running and starting requirements. That is why we assess the actual appliances instead of relying on a generic package.

Do I have to remove my generator?

Not necessarily. Many businesses keep a generator as another backup source. The system can be designed so solar and batteries carry more of the load while the generator is retained where useful.

Can we install without shutting down the supermarket?

Installation is planned around the site and operating requirements. Any necessary interruption is discussed in advance so disruption can be minimised.

How large a solar system do I need?

It depends on your refrigeration, lighting, checkout equipment, operating hours, desired backup duration and which loads you want solar to support. The assessment determines this.

How much does the system cost?

Commercial solar pricing depends heavily on load size, battery capacity, inverter requirements and installation conditions. We first assess the supermarket, then recommend a system and quote based on the actual scope.

Do you provide after-sales support?

Yes. The project includes system handover and an agreed post-installation support arrangement.
